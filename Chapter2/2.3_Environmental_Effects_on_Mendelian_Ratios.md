## 2.3 Environmental Effects on Mendelian Ratios

Mendel's theory posited that the phenotype is exclusively determined by the genotype value, exhibiting a ratio of 3:1. This finding aligns with the theoretical framework of complete penetrance of dominance genotype. However, environmental factors may cause a deviation from the expected Mendelian ratio due to incomplete penetrance of the genotype. For the purposes of this discussion, it is proposed that the red flower is the reference phenotype and the recessive trait is the white flower. Furthermore, the penetrance of AA and Aa is designated as α, while the penetrance of aa is defined as 1 - β. Table 2.2 illustrate the conditional probability table. The phenotypic ratio can be expressed as 
$$[\frac{3}{4}α+\frac{1}{4}(1-β)]:[\frac{1}{4}(1-α)+\frac{3}{4}β]$$
. It must be noted that this ratio operates under the assumption of a dominant A over a, and that AA and Aa are equal, without incorporating the effects discussed in Section 2.2, such as codominance. 

Table 2.2 The conditional probability table.

|Genetype|Red Flower|White Flower|Marginal|
|:------:|:--------:|:----------:|:------:|
|AA|α|1 - α|$\frac{1}{4}$|
|Aa|α|1 - α|$\frac{1}{2}$|
|aa|1 - β|β|$\frac{1}{4}$|
|Marginal|$\frac{3}{4}$α + $\frac{1}{4}$(1 - β)|$\frac{1}{4}$(1 - α) + $\frac{3}{4}$β||
